How it works

Three steps. No surprises.

  1. 1

    You set the terms

    Name the goal, the quality bar and a hard budget. When the budget is spent, the agents stop and wait.

  2. 2

    Agents work, a judge measures

    They test fixes on your own code. A separate judge measures every one against the bar you fixed in advance.

  3. 3

    Your engineers approve

    Only verified savings arrive, as changes in your own repositories. Nothing ships on its own.

Trust

Agents propose. A judge decides. You approve.

The judge is independent of the agents and never grades their work on their terms. Every saving comes with a signed record.
