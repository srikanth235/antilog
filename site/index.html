The problem

Everyone has agents now. Few can say what they returned.

Agents produce endless work: code, changes, ideas. Activity is easy to count. Whether the business got better is not. Antilog starts from the outcome and works back.

How it works

You set the outcome. Agents do the work. Antilog shows the return.

  1. 1

    Set the outcome

    Say what should improve, how it is measured, what each unit of improvement is worth, and how much you are willing to spend.

  2. 2

    Agents do the work

    They run experiment after experiment on your code and data, on their own, inside your budget.

  3. 3

    See the return

    Each improvement is measured independently and arrives for your approval with its value in dollars.

Why you can trust the figure
