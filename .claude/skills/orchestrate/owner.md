# The owner

Product calls go to the owner. A product call is anything the [product](../product/SKILL.md) skill does not settle: a non-goal to reverse, a new promise, a new dependency or outside system, the open questions in [rulings](../product/rulings.md).

- Put each call as options. Give a recommendation and say what each option costs.
- Ask one call at a time, with the evidence.
- Do not decide a product call by implementing it, and do not tell a session to.
- Report to the owner in plain words: what landed, what it changes for a user, what was not verified, what is owed. Link [land/report.md](../land/report.md).
- When the owner decides, record it in the product skill's `rulings.md` in the same change.
