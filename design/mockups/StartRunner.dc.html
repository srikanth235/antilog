Infrastructure

Start a runner

The runner runs in your infrastructure and connects out over HTTPS. It needs no inbound port, and Ratchet holds no access to your cloud.

Registration token · shown once
rrt_example_0000000000000000
Expires in 1 hour. Ratchet keeps only a fingerprint, so it cannot show this again.
helm install ratchet-runner ratchet/runner \
  --namespace ratchet --create-namespace \
  --set server=https://acme.ratchet.cloud \
  --set token=rrt_example_0000000000000000
Waiting for the runner to connect
A connected runner appears in Infrastructure.
